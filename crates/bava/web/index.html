Plays the video below and captures this tab in one go — your browser will ask you to confirm the share. Clicking the visualizer or pasting a YouTube link anywhere does the same thing.

In the share dialog, pick a tab and make sure “Also share tab audio” is on — a window or whole screen carries no audio on Linux or macOS. You can switch which tab is being shared from the browser's own sharing bar, without coming back here.
