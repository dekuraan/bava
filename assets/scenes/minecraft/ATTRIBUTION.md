# Minecraft scene assets — attribution

Everything in this folder is **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**. No Mojang/Minecraft
assets are used; the block textures are an original CC0 set drawn for a voxel game
("16x16 Block Texture Set" by ARoachIFoundOnMyPillow, Comfy52 palette), and the recolors below
stay inside that same palette. Credit is not required by CC0 but is given here anyway.

License verified (2026-09-28) from: the OpenGameArt page's *License(s): CC0* field;
the `License.txt` inside each Kenney zip ("License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0)"),
and each kenney.nl asset page ("Creative Commons CC0").

## Atlas layout (`textures/blocks.png`, 128×128, 16 px tiles, 8 per row)

| Row (y px) | col 0 | col 1 | col 2 | col 3 | col 4 | col 5 | col 6 | col 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (0–15) | 0 `grass_top` | 1 `grass_side` | 2 `dirt` | 3 `stone` | 4 `cobblestone` | 5 `sand` | 6 `water` | 7 `snow` |
| 1 (16–31) | 8 `log_side` | 9 `log_top` | 10 `leaves` | 11 `planks` | 12 `gravel` | 13 `bedrock` | 14 `glowstone` | 15 `lava` |
| 2 (32–47) | 16 `diamond_ore` | 17 `gold_ore` | 18 `emerald_ore` | 19 `redstone_ore` | 20 `coal_ore` | 21 `iron_ore` | 22 `obsidian` | 23 `glass` |

Slots 24–63 are fully transparent. `glass` has a transparent interior (alpha 0/255 only);
every other tile is fully opaque. Substitutions: `bedrock` = the set's *basalt* (dark
stone); `glowstone` = the set's *sand_ugly_2* (bright yellow blotches, used unmodified);
`stone` = *stone_generic*; `sand`, `water`, `lava`, `emerald_ore`, `redstone_ore`,
`coal_ore`, `iron_ore` are palette recolors of tiles from the same set (details below).
